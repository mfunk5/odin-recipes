Simple Macaroni and Cheese

Tip
Gather the ingredients: portioning out your ingredients before you begin lets you take stock of anything missing and keeps the project organized.

Description

This macaroni and cheese is made with 7 ingredients you probably already have at home, and comes together in just 25 minutes.

Perfect for a weeknight, it is also adaptable if you have more time and want to throw it in the oven with additional toppings or add-ins.

Store leftovers in the fridge for three to four days, or freeze them for up to six months. Reheat with a splash of milk on the stove or in the microwave.

Ingredients

  • 1 (8oz) box elbow macaroni
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • ground black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese
Spice it up
Some people enjoy adding a pinch of ground mustard, turmeric, or cayenne pepper to their cheese sauce as it cooks.

Steps

  1. Cook the macaroni: bring a large pot of salted water to a boil before adding elbow macaroni. Stir occasionally. Cook for 8 minutes, or until pasta is tender to your liking.
  2. Melt butter: while macaroni is cooking, melt butter in a saucepan. This is the beginning of your sauce.
  3. Add flour, salt, and pepper into melted butter: whisk until the mixture is smooth and to keep the flour from burning while it browns. Allow to cook for about five minutes.
  4. Pour Milk: slowly pour milk into mixure while stirring. Cook and stir the mixture until it is smooth and bubbling.
  5. Add Cheddar: whisk in the cheese and stir until melted, two to four minutes.
  6. Add drained macaroni: drain and add the macaroni until it is coated in cheese sauce.
  7. Serve and enjoy or transfer to an oven safe pan for baking.
