Simple Macaroni and Cheese

photo by Shaolanda Isbell, recipe by g0dluvsugly

Description

This macaroni and cheese is made with 7 ingredients you probably already have at home, and comes together in just 25 minutes.

Perfect for a weeknight, it is also adaptable if you have more time and want to throw it in the oven with additional toppings or add-ins.

Store leftovers in the fridge for three to four days, or freeze them for up to six months. Reheat with a splash of milk on the stove or in the microwave.

Ingredients

  • 1 (8oz) box elbow macaroni
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • ground black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients: portioning out your ingredients before you begin lets you take stock of anything missing and keeps the project organized.
  2. Cook the macaroni: bring a large pot of salted water to a boil before adding elbow macaroni. Stir occasionally. Cook for 8 minutes, or until pasta is tender to your liking.
  3. Melt butter: while macaroni is cooking, melt butter in a saucepan. This is the beginning of your sauce.
  4. Add flour, salt, and pepper into melted butter: whisk until the mixture is smooth and to keep the flour from burning while it browns. Allow to cook for about five minutes.
  5. Pour Milk: slowly pour milk into mixure while stirring. Cook and stir the mixture until it is smooth and bubbling, another five minutes or so.
  6. Add Cheddar: whisk in the cheese and stir until melted, two to four minutes.
  7. Add drained macaroni: drain the macaroni if you haven't already, and add it to your cheese sauce. Stir until all the macaroni is coated.
  8. Serve and enjoy or transfer to an oven safe pan for baking. This is also when you can add any add-ins.
