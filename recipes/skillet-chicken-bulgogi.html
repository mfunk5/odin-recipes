Skillet Chicken Bulgogi

photo by Christine C., recipe inspired by daveparks4

Description

This asian inspired recipe calls for chicken, but can easily be made with beef or pork. Additional vegetables can be added to your liking.

This recipe comes together in 30 minutes with a little prep and can be served over rice, or with lettuce leaves.

Marinate the meat overnight for a stronger flavor. Double the marinade if you enjoy extra sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup finely chopped onion
  • 5 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon ginger
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 lb skinless, boneless chicken breasts cut into thin strips
  • Optional add-ins: snow peas, water chestnuts, carrot straws, sliced mushrooms, broccoli florets
  • Optional: 1/2 tablespoon cornstarch and 1/4 cup water for cornstarch slurry

Steps

  1. Make the marinade: whisk onion, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, cayenne pepper, salt and black pepper together in a bowl until smooth.
  2. Optional if pre-marinating: add thinly cut chicken strips to marinade and cover in the fridge for a few hours or overnight. Skip this step if you've only got thirty minutes.
  3. Drain chicken and keep marinade: meat browns best when it is not wet.
  4. Cook chicken in skillet: use medium-high heat for about 15 minutes until chicken is cooked through. If you've skipped marinating the chicken, you should salt and pepper at this stage. Flip or stir chicken and watch for browning.
  5. Add marinade with chicken and allow marinade to reduce or thicken marinade with cornstarch slurry.
  6. Meanwhile, cook any vegetable add-ins: vegetables like broccoli cook faster than chicken and release juices that will affect how well the chicken browns.
  7. Add vegetables to chicken and sauce. Garnish with sesame seeds.
  8. Serve over rice or inside lettuce leaves.
