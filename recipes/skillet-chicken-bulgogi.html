Fun Fact
Bulgogi literally translates to "Fire Beef" in Korean and is traditionally grilled meat.

Skillet Chicken Bulgogi

photo by Christine C., recipe inspired by daveparks4
Vegetables
Veggies like snow peas, water chestnuts, carrot straws, broccoli florets or sliced mushrooms pair well with this dish.

Description

This Korean recipe calls for chicken, but can easily be made with beef or pork. Additional vegetables can be added to your liking. This recipe comes together in 30 minutes with a little prep and can be served over rice, or with lettuce leaves. Marinate the meat overnight for a stronger flavor. Double the marinade if you enjoy extra sauce.

Ingredients

Thickening Sauce
To make a cornstarch slurry add 1/2 tablespoon cornstarch and 1/4 cup water

Marinade:

  • 1/4 cup finely chopped onion
  • 5 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon ginger
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 lb skinless, boneless chicken breasts cut into thin strips

Steps

Overnight Marinade
Longer marinades produce more flavorful meats. Try marinating overnight in the fridge.
  1. Make the marinade: whisk marinade ingredients together in a bowl.
  2. Marinade the chicken for at least 15 minutes.
  3. Drain chicken and keep marinade: gently pat dry
  4. Cook chicken in skillet: use medium-high heat 8-15 minutes until chicken is cooked through. Flip or stir chicken and watch for browning.
  5. Re-add marinade with chicken and allow marinade to reduce or thicken marinade with cornstarch slurry.
  6. Meanwhile, cook any vegetable add-ins: vegetables like broccoli cook faster than chicken and release juices that will affect how well the chicken browns.
  7. Add vegetables to chicken and sauce. Garnish with sesame seeds.
  8. Serve over rice or inside lettuce leaves.
